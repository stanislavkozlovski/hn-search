# [Show HN: Web app that shows how good the air quality is in Chile](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=42505796)

[[./thread|Discussion]]

## Description

Discussion responds to a Chilean air quality web app built on government sensor data. Participants ask about historical records, extending the app to other countries, and discrepancies between readings from different sources; they also comment on seasonal smog in cities such as Temuco and Santiago and suggest OpenAQ as a resource.
Tags: Chile, air quality app, sensor data, historical data, air pollution

## Relevance

Comments discuss winter smog associated with diesel vehicles and wood burning, and a disagreement between air quality readings from different sources that may reflect timing or sensor quality. ## Arguments

- erikw [[./thread#^c42526195|erikw]] argues that seasonal context matters: Temuco and Santiago become very smoggy in winter, which they associate with basin geography, diesel vehicles, and wood-burning stoves. This is a qualitative local claim without pollutant concentrations or an explicitly described personal measurement.
- dietr1ch [[./thread#^c42526526|dietr1ch]] proposes historical playback to show changes over time and standardized station coordinates and sensor fields to broaden coverage. iwayato [[./thread#^c42540439|iwayato]] considers international integration possible if public data exists; toomuchtodo [[./thread#^c42527029|toomuchtodo]] recommends OpenAQ. These are data-access suggestions, without demonstrated improvements in measurement accuracy.

## Counterarguments

- FreePalestine1 [[./thread#^c42528455|FreePalestine1]] challenges the app’s apparent assessment of Chiuchiu: it appears red while Google reportedly says the air is fine. This supplies a conflicting indicator, but does not establish which source better represents actual exposure.

## Conditions

- The geographic explanation is specific to Temuco and Santiago, which erikw [[./thread#^c42526195|erikw]] describes as sitting in bowls with substantial diesel and wood-stove emissions. In reply, mickelsen [[./thread#^c42526517|mickelsen]] says Temuco can become worse than Beijing, explicitly limiting that extreme comparison to peak winter. Neither comment supports a year-round or nationwide characterization.
- Extending the app beyond Chile depends on accessible public data and compatible formatting, according to iwayato [[./thread#^c42540439|iwayato]] in response to dietr1ch [[./thread#^c42526526|dietr1ch]]. The discussion provides no financial estimates for monitoring or exposure reduction.
