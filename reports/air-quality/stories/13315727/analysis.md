## Description

A brief discussion about severe air pollution in parts of Asia and possible ways to address it. Replies distinguish reducing pollution at its source, attempting to clear or redirect polluted air across a city, and protecting people in smaller spaces with purifiers or masks. Participants wonder whether large-scale air movement or rainmaking could help, mention weather modification, and make a pop-culture reference to bottled clean air.
Tags: air-pollution, Asia, air-quality, public-policy, air-purification, weather-modification

## Relevance

The discussion distinguishes personal exposure control through domestic purifiers or masks from broader air-cleaning ideas, and considers whether rain removes pollutants. These are mostly speculative proposals rather than firsthand measurements or tested improvements. ## Arguments

- rahimnathwani [[./thread#^c13315985|rahimnathwani]] distinguishes reducing emissions, cleaning or redirecting ambient pollution, and cleaning the small space people breathe. He identifies domestic purifiers and masks as options individuals can control, but provides no effectiveness data.
- rahimnathwani [[./thread#^c13315985|rahimnathwani]] asks whether a huge fan could move polluted air out of a city. Replying within that discussion of ambient-air interventions, ddorian43 [[./thread#^c13317007|ddorian43]] proposes chemically inducing rain from aircraft. Both are exploratory suggestions.
- pizza [[./thread#^c13316913|pizza]] proposes using an apartment lobby as an air lock, without explaining its operation or reporting an exposure reduction.
- joeclark77 [[./thread#^c13317408|joeclark77]] invokes Spaceballs’ “Perri-Air” humorously; this supplies neither a concrete intervention nor evidence of cleaner personal air.

## Counterarguments

- rahimnathwani [[./thread#^c13317709|rahimnathwani]] qualifies his own belief that China induces rain for important foreign visitors by acknowledging that the apparent pattern could be coincidence. This is self-expressed uncertainty, not an independently corroborated account or another participant’s rebuttal.

## Assumptions

- ddorian43 [[./thread#^c13317007|ddorian43]] bases the rainmaking suggestion on rain removing pollutants, explicitly asking whether that premise is correct. rahimnathwani [[./thread#^c13317709|rahimnathwani]] answers affirmatively but supplies no pollutant-specific evidence or measurements.
- rahimnathwani [[./thread#^c13315985|rahimnathwani]] treats personal purification as a route to cleaner inhaled air without distinguishing pollutant types. His comment does not establish which devices address PM2.5, NO2 or CO2, or how effectively.

## Conditions

- rahimnathwani [[./thread#^c13315985|rahimnathwani]] links emission reduction, illustrated by reducing factories, to GDP and government policy. This is his asserted economic and institutional constraint on individual action; he gives no household costs or affordability comparisons.
- rahimnathwani’s China example [[./thread#^c13317709|rahimnathwani]] concerns alleged rainmaking around foreign dignitary visits. Its geographic and situational specificity, together with his explicit uncertainty, limits its usefulness for ordinary household exposure decisions.
