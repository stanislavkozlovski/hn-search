  - **vel0city** · [2023-10-17 17:25 UTC](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=37918437)
    I wouldn't recommend using a gas stove if you don't have a good way to ventilate. ^c37918437

    My backup cooking in case of some long term power failure is a two burner propane camping stove along with a few camp-sized cylinders of propane. That plus I usually have a small pile of firewood and at least 20lbs of charcoal for the grill. If the power goes out for any lengthy period of time I'll be cooking outside.

    As for making your current stove better, ensure you actually have a decently strong vent hood that will actually ventilate outside. You'll need to size the blower enough to actually force air out of the house which will vary based on how far the air needs to travel. If your fan is too weak it won't be able to adequately push the air outside at any useful rate.

        A well-sized range hood vent fan is going to be your first line of defense though.
