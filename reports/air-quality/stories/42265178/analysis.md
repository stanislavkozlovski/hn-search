## Description

The creator presents an open-source Delhi air-pollution tool that converts AQI readings into cigarette-equivalent exposure and estimated life expectancy effects. Discussion questions the reliability and framing of those equivalencies, and the creator provides a source and agrees to remove a disputed practical-advice section. Participants also report a mobile-browser failure and ask what local authorities are doing. Proposed responses such as water sprinklers and cloud seeding are viewed skeptically, with discussion identifying crop-residue burning, traffic, and industry as major sources.
Tags: air pollution, Delhi, AQI, public health, open-source tools, product feedback

- Interpreting an outdoor AQI figure as an individual’s cigarette-equivalent daily exposure requires assumptions about the index, underlying pollutant concentrations, exposure duration, and health-risk conversion. The exchange between profsummergig [[./thread#^c42265868|profsummergig]] and namanyayg [[./thread#^c42266504|namanyayg]] does not specify these inputs or account for differences in residents’ indoor and outdoor exposure.
