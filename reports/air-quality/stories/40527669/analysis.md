## Arguments

- tbeseda [[./thread#^c40530798|tbeseda]] highlights fragmented access to TEMPO information: instrument documentation explains data levels, ASDC describes formats, and Earthdata appears to provide storage and visualization. This concerns making observations usable; it supplies no actual air-quality readings.
- Responding to rufus\_foreman's request for a local real-time map [[./thread#^c40530806|rufus\_foreman]], tbeseda [[./thread#^c40531047|tbeseda]] recommends PurpleAir, toomuchtodo [[./thread#^c40565760|toomuchtodo]] links OpenAQ, and adultSwim [[./thread#^c40563392|adultSwim]] links AirNow. These are suggested monitoring resources, without comparisons of accuracy, pollutant coverage, or suitability for personal exposure decisions.

## Counterarguments

- tbeseda [[./thread#^c40531047|tbeseda]] cautions that a real-time map may not yet be available from this dataset. Their wording is tentative: the announcement of near-real-time data does not itself establish an accessible neighborhood map.
