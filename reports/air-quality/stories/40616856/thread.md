    It is. Canada is covered in forests that sit on very thick layers of peat. Thanks to climate change we've entered a death spiral where the climate dries out the peat enough to make it flammable and once it catches fire, the heat further dries it out which makes deeper peat flammable the next season. The layer is at least a few meters deep and up to tens of meters deep in some places so the fires can even burn underground between seasons, leading to neverending wildfires. ^c40618420

    Russia is facing the same problem with their peat and sooner or later, the strip from Washington to Montana will face it too.
