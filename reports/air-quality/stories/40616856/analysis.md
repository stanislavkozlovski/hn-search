## Description

Discussion around a wildfire-smoke event covers its unusual impact on the eastern United States, how people in regions accustomed to smoke perceive the news coverage, and whether the event signals a wider trend. Participants share observations of haze and discuss worsening fire seasons, dry conditions, and smoke transport. A branch describes a proposed cycle in which drying peat becomes more combustible, fires burn underground or recur, and stored carbon is released. Another branch shifts to ESRI and GIS software, correcting confusion between proprietary ArcGIS and open-source QGIS.
