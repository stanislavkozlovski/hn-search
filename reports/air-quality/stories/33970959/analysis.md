# [\$949 price for Dyson air-purifying headphones more absurd than device itself](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=33970959)

[[./thread|Discussion]]

## Description

Discussion largely questions the value and design of Dyson's air-purifying headphones, including whether an unsealed device can filter gases and whether airflow might spread droplets toward others. Some see possible use in polluted environments, while others criticize the product as expensive consumerism. The thread also includes broader complaints about Dyson products and battery design.
Tags: air purifier, headphones, product design, consumer products, pathogens

## Relevance

The discussion considers whether wearable filtration could protect someone in polluted environments, questions claims about gas filtration without a face seal, and raises a possible tradeoff involving pathogen droplets. It offers opinions rather than performance measurements. [[./thread#^c33972278|GekkePrutser]] [[./thread#^c33973132|joezydeco]] [[./thread#^c33973204|cwkoss]] [[./thread#^c33974101|error503]]

## Arguments

- error503 [[./thread#^c33974101|error503]] sees possible value in wearable air filtration where pollution is severe, while questioning its usefulness against pathogens. This is a conditional opinion, with no reported exposure reduction.
- Replying directly to the objection about an incomplete facial seal, joezydeco [[./thread#^c33973132|joezydeco]] asks whether positive pressure around the nose and mouth could make sealing unnecessary. This proposes a mechanism; it does not establish that the headphones produce effective protection.

## Counterarguments

- GekkePrutser [[./thread#^c33972278|GekkePrutser]] questions how an unsealed device could deliver its claimed gas filtration and dismisses the product as 'snake oil'. The objection concerns the design and advertised capability, without a firsthand test of these headphones.
- cwkoss [[./thread#^c33973204|cwkoss]] predicts that the airflow would project the wearer's microdroplets farther, potentially providing marginal protection to the wearer while increasing nearby people's pathogen exposure. No airflow, droplet-dispersion, or infection measurements support that prediction in this unit.

## Assumptions

- The mechanism suggested by joezydeco [[./thread#^c33973132|joezydeco]] depends on the device actually maintaining useful positive pressure around the breathing zone. The harm predicted by cwkoss [[./thread#^c33973204|cwkoss]] depends on its airflow increasing dispersal of potentially infectious droplets. Neither condition is demonstrated.
- GekkePrutser [[./thread#^c33972278|GekkePrutser]] refers to unspecified 'gases', and error503 [[./thread#^c33974101|error503]] discusses pollution generally. Treating either comment as evidence of effectiveness against PM2.5, NO2, or CO2 would require unsupported assumptions about the pollutants and filtration performance.

## Conditions
